It's a little after 11. The Venue Barn is still fully lit up, but empty. An hour before, the Wedding Party and Guests grabbed their belongings, chatted a few minutes and then headed home, or back to their hotel or AIRBnB. The Wedding Couple and parents thanked Cindy and Krista for everything, and also hit the road. Then… quiet. No horrible wedding dance songs to torture old men and titillate young women. Raucous laughter from obnoxious groomsmen was to be heard no more this night.

The gentle lapping of waves against the breakwater could be heard clearly, a comforting, fuzzy kind of noise. The fields were blanketed with hundreds of lightening bugs, all looking to get another dance in. A werewolf howled in the distance.

Cindy returned from our house, led by the two dogs leaving neon red and green light traces in the now darkened yard. The decorations were packed and placed inside the barn for tomorrow's pickup. Cindy, Krista and I cleared off tables and chairs, and swept the floors clean. The dogs helped, by eating any cupcake crumbs they could find. The remaining bagged trash was loaded on my trailer, Cindy and Krista called it a night, I went to the work barn to finish up.

It took a little while, but we were ready for tomorrow's tours. 

Good night,

Jack
