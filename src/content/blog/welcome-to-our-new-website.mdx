I am so excited to welcome you to our brand-new website for Lower Notley Hall Farm!

After many years of working on this venue website, I've updated things to make it easier than ever to learn about the venue, explore your options, and book a private tour.

## What's New

* **[Interactive booking calendar](/tours/)** - See real-time availability and request a tour slot directly online.
* **[Pricing Estimator](/pricing/)** — Customize your package and get an instant estimate, then print or save it as a PDF.
* **[Updated photographs](/about/)** — Explore high-resolution galleries of the Manor House, Reception Barn, Wicomico River waterfront, and more.

I hope this new experience helps you envision your perfect day at Lower Notley Hall Farm. As always, [Jack and Cindy are just a phone call or email away](/contact/) if you have any questions.
