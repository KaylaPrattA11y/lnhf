Nearing mid-summer, the rush is on to get into the garden and the orchard and pick, pull and sort through all of the fruits and vegetables. Cindy enjoys the quiet morning with Dol and Notley in tow. Peppers, tomatoes, and some carrots are ready. The mosquitoes are ready too. The gates to the garden have to be closed all the time now, as the chickens will not hesitate to destroy the tomatoes. A neighboring farmer and friend has quietly slipped by and left us a bushel of sweet corn! Canning time, and corn shucks for the goats!

Our daughter (and website creator!), son-in-law and children have also come to take their ‘harvest’ from the river. He is pulling in perch while the kids help Mom catch some crabs for dinner. Chicken and clam scraps are thrown in the river and the gulls come from out of nowhere for dinner. Meanwhile Grandpa shakes his fist at a passing jet-skier and hoarsely yells ‘get off my river’. 

Soon we will try to plant a batch of wildflowers along the lane, that will provide a harvest for the Golden Finches, sparrows and other tiny flying critters. The blooms will be seen this fall, if Mother Nature helps us out. Maybe some of the flowers can be used to decorate at Wedding Ceremonies and Receptions here.
