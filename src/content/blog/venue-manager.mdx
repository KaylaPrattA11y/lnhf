Hello! I'm Lynsey, the new Venue Manager and In-House Wedding Coordinator at Lower Notley Hall Farm!

I am beyond excited to officially introduce myself and share a little bit about my journey!

My love for the wedding industry started in 2022 when I got married. During the planning process, I completely fell in love with everything that goes into creating such a special day. From the little details to watching all the planning come together, I knew I wanted to be part of making those memories for other couples.

That passion led me to open my own wedding coordination business, As You Wish by Lynsey LLC, in 2023. Since then, I have had the pleasure of working with so many wonderful couples, coordinating weddings, and helping bring their visions to life. I have also had the opportunity to work weddings at Lower Notley Hall Farm, and it didn't take long for this beautiful venue to become a place I truly love.

Now, I am so excited to take the next step and officially become part of the Lower Notley Hall Farm family as your Venue Manager and In-House Wedding Coordinator!

I won't be doing it alone! Alongside me will be my husband, Billy, who will be taking on the role of Venue Operations Manager. He will be helping with parking, maintenance, event setup, heavy lifting, and all the behind-the-scenes logistics that help keep everything running smoothly. Together, we are excited to be a team that makes your wedding experience as seamless and enjoyable as possible!

![](/uploads/BH-1.jpg)

I truly believe that your wedding venue should feel like more than just a place to get married. It should feel welcoming, personal, and special from the moment you book until the moment you walk down the aisle.

I am so grateful for this opportunity and cannot wait to meet future couples, work alongside amazing vendors, and be part of so many beautiful wedding days.

If you are considering Lower Notley Hall Farm for your wedding, I cannot wait to welcome you and help make your vision come to life!

Here's to a new chapter, new beginnings, and many beautiful celebrations ahead!

With love,

Lynsey Halbritter

Venue Manager & In-House Wedding Coordinator
