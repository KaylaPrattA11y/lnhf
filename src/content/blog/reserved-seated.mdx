Soon guests will be arriving, looking for a good seat at a Lower Notley Hall Farm Southern Maryland wedding ceremony. 

A Mourning Dove is our early bird. She has picked out the best seat on the Manor House for her and her chicks to watch this fall's upcoming ceremonies. The Osage Orange trees have filled in and supply a big, beautiful shady spot for all of our wedding guests.

We found a Box Turtle headed down the driveway towards the Manor house. Planning ahead to beat the last-minute rush.
