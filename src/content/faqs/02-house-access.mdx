The Manor House includes multiple elegant spaces available for your event: the Double Parlors, the Garner Dining Room, and the Locke Rooms. Each space offers distinct historic character. The covered porch overlooking the Wicomico River is available for cocktail hours and gatherings.

Please ask us during your tour about specific room availability for your event type and size.
