question: "How many guests will LNHF accommodate?"
sortOrder: 6

Lower Notley Hall Farm can comfortably accommodate up to **150 guests** for a combined indoor/outdoor event. For events with fewer than 100 guests, a pricing discount applies — see our [Pricing page](/pricing/) for details.

For specific layout questions or larger party inquiries, please [contact us](/contact) and we'll discuss the best arrangement for your event.
