question: "What does Lower Notley Hall Farm provide besides the location?"
sortOrder: 3
---

The base venue rental includes:

- Use of restrooms throughout the property
- Tables, chairs, and benches
- Firepit
- Decorative accessories already on-site: wine barrels, some linens, a portable bar, cake tables, buffet tables, mason jars, shepherd hooks, and your choice of two wedding arches

The base price does **not** include a bartender, DJ, cake, caterer, wedding planner, photographers, or similar vendor services.
